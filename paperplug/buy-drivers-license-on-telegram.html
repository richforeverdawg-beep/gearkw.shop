Why a licence is harder than an ID

A state ID carries a name, a photo and a number. A licence carries all of that plus a class code, sometimes endorsement and restriction codes, sometimes an organ-donor mark, and an expiry that follows a state-specific rule tied to your age. There are more fields to get wrong and each one is visible at a glance to anyone who holds a licence from that state.

So we treat the class line as the first question, not an afterthought. Send the class and the rest follows from it.

Endorsements and restrictions

CDL cards print endorsement letters (H hazmat, N tanker, P passenger, S school bus, T doubles) and restriction codes. We print what you ask for, but the set has to be plausible: an N without an H on a hazmat-heavy card, or a school-bus S with no P, reads wrong. Tell us the work you do and we will set a coherent line.

Restrictions also cover glasses and hearing aids. If your photo shows glasses, the corrective-lens restriction should be on the card. Small detail, easy to miss, very visible.

The magnetic strip

Every card ships with a black high-coercivity strip applied, because its absence is visible on the back. Encoding it so a reader returns your data is a $20 add-on. The strip is encoded to the AAMVA track format with your licence number and DOB in the right fields.

A strip that is present but blank is worse than no strip, because a swipe at a rental desk produces nothing. If you expect to hand the card to a clerk who swipes, take the encoding.

Expiry and layout

Expiry is computed from your issue date by that state's rule. Most states issue 4, 6 or 8 years; a handful issue to a birthday. Under-21 holders get the vertical or marked layout. Over-65 holders sometimes get a shorter term. We set all three from the DOB rather than letting you pick, unless you ask.

Licence vs ID vs Real ID

A state ID is $25 cheaper and does the same job at a bar. A licence adds the driving entitlement, which matters if you are asked to show it at a rental counter or a job that checks. A Real ID adds the gold star, which matters for flights. Pick on the use, not the price.
