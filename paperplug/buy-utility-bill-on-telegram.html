PDF or printed

Two formats, same price, different jobs.

A PDF and a print are not interchangeable. If you upload a photo of a printed bill to a portal, it looks like a photo of paper — often fine, sometimes rejected. If a teller asks for paper, a PDF on your phone is a second look. Say which one you need and stop guessing.

Printed copies go out on 90gsm white with a folded insert envelope so the creases land naturally. Not stapled flat — a bill that has clearly never been folded is a small tell.

Providers

Any major US or UK energy, water, broadband or council provider. Send the provider name and the bill style — paper statement, online statement PDF, or a reminder notice — and we confirm the layout before quoting. Layouts differ: a Con Edison statement and a Thames Water reminder look nothing alike, and a generic template with a logo pasted on is the usual weak point in this product.

Regional co-ops and municipal utilities are fine too, just slower to confirm.

Address matching

Send the address copied from your ID, not retyped. Abbreviation style matters — "Apt 4B" against "Unit 4B" against "#4B" is the kind of mismatch a clerk catches while you stand there.

If the bill is the only document you own, that is fine; just tell us the address you want and we will set it without an ID to match. If you are building a stack, order the ID in the same DM and everything comes out consistent.

What gets accepted, honestly

  • Usually accepted: letting agent reference, gym, phone contract, parcel collection, school admin, most counter checks.
  • Sometimes accepted: bank address change, council tax setup, credit checks at a counter.
  • Not accepted as proof: anything that queries a live utility database — a supplier switch, some credit applications.

Bills dated within the last 90 days read as normal. Older ones get questioned. We date them to the current cycle unless you ask otherwise.
