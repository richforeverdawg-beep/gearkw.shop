Why the age of a vendor matters more than its reviews

A Telegram vendor can manufacture reviews in an afternoon. A vendor cannot manufacture a channel history with dates spread over nine years, complaints that were left up, and a price list that was the same in 2023 as it is now.

That is what this page is for. Not a story — a record you can scroll.

What we are not

Not a state authority. Not a marketplace with a hundred sellers. Not affiliated with any DMV, passport office or the SSA. There is one person answering and a small print bench behind them, and the whole operation is deliberately that size because quality control collapses above it.

If you want a cart, a checkout and an order number, this will annoy you. If you want to talk to the person making the thing, it will not.
