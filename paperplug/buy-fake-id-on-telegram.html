State coverage and layout rules

Every state is available. Templates are not one design recoloured 50 times — portrait position, seal, header typeface and licence-number format differ per state and we build to each. Two rules matter more than the rest:

  • Under-21 layouts. A dozen states print vertical or landscape with an under-21 marker. If your date of birth puts you at 20, a horizontal 21+ layout is a tell. We set the orientation from the DOB automatically.
  • Issue and expiry dates. Expiry is calculated from your issue date by that state's rule, usually 4, 6 or 8 years. A California card expiring in 4 years when California issues 8-year cards is the single most common flaw in cheap IDs.

Bring the DOB you want and the address of a real street in that state — not a PO box, not a UPS Store. If you have no address, say so in the DM and we will use one that matches the county on the card.
