About PaperPlug

Two people, one print bench, a Telegram handle. Trading since 2017 and deliberately not bigger than this.

What we do

We make identification documents and proof-of-address paper. State IDs, Real IDs, driver's licenses, passports, SSN cards, utility bills. Flower too, since 2025, because the same buyers kept asking and it was silly to send them elsewhere.

Everything is sold in a Telegram DM. There is no cart, no checkout and no account, and there will not be.

Why no storefront

A storefront is a layer between you and the person making the thing. It adds a cart, a plugin, a payment processor and an order number, and it removes the only two things that matter here: a proof before payment and a human who remembers your order.

We tried a shop in 2021 for four months. Order quality fell, because people filled in fields and stopped reading. The DM forces a conversation, and the conversation is where errors get caught.

The bench

One person answers every DM. One person runs the printer, the laminator and the binder. Same two people since 2019. Nothing is outsourced except postage, and PDF417 encoding was brought in-house in 2022 after too many inconsistent third-party files.

Capacity is about 40 documents a day. Above that, quality drops and we would rather queue than drop it. If the queue is long you are told the wait before you pay, not after.

Standards

  • Proof before payment, always
  • 50/50 split, balance after tracking
  • Published fixed prices, held
  • Free reprint inside 14 days on a defect
  • Limits published on every product page rather than buried
  • Complaints left in the channel with the resolution under them

Those six are the whole operation. Everything else is detail.

What we are not

Not a government authority. Not a marketplace with a hundred sellers under one banner. Not affiliated with any DMV, passport office or the Social Security Administration. Our documents are replicas on real materials, and they do not resolve against a live record. Pages say so where it matters.
